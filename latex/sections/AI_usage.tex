\chapter{Use of AI tools}

This chapter documents how generative AI tools (large language models) were used during the development of
Task~\#15 and Task~\#47, in compliance with the course policy on AI-assisted work. The tools were used as a coding and
writing assistant; every result was reviewed and validated by the author.

\noindent In Task~\#15, AI was used mainly as a coding tool, where every part of the code was specifically tuned
for the needs I (human) required. 

\noindent On the other hand, in Task~\#47 I wanted to explore more deeply the world of agentic AI: I used an Opencode
environment specifically tuned to work with the project constraints given by the professor. This gave me full control
of the \emph{ideas} behind the code, while losing direct control of the code itself. At the same time, I could
invest more energy in the concepts and difficult mathematical theories behind this project.

\noindent In both tasks, AI was used as a writing assistant, to help correct systax and typos.
